Butter Chicken Recipe

Ingredients

    For the Chicken Marinade:

  • Chicken: 500g-600g, boneless breast or thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces.
  • Yogurt: 1/2 cup (Greek yogurt works best for thickness).
  • Spices: 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder (for color), 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder.
  • Acid: 1 tbsp lemon juice.
  • Salt: To taste.

    For the Makhani Grevy:

  • Butter: 3-4 tbsp (use unsalted).
  • Oil: 1-2 tbsp (to prevent butter from burning).
  • Aromatics: 1 medium onion (finely dices), 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste.
  • Tomato Base: 500g tomatos (or 1 can of tomato puree/passata).
  • Spices: 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp cumin powder.
  • Finishing Touches: 3-4 tbsp heavy cream, 1 tsp kasoori methi (dried fenugreek leaves, crushed), and sugar (1 tsp, to balance acidity).

Step-by-Step Recipe

  1. Mrinate the Chicken

    2. In a bowl, mix the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and all the marinade spices. Add the chicken pieces, ensuring they are well coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to 2 hours (overnight is best for extra tenderness).

  2. Cook the Chicken

    3. Heat a pan with a little oil over medium-high heat. Add the chicken pieces (don't crowd the pan). Sear the chicken until it’s browned on all sides and mostly cooked through. Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside.

  3. Prepare the Base

    4. In the same pan, melt the butter with a splash of oil. Add the finely diced onions and sauté until they are soft and translucent. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute until fragrant.

  4. Simmer the Sauce

    5. Add the tomatoes (or purée) and the chili powder, garam masala, and cumin. Let this simmer on medium-low heat for about 10-15 minutes until the oil starts to separate from the sides of the mixture.

    - Tip: If you want an ultra-smooth, restaurant-style texture, let this mixture cool slightly and blend it into a fine puree before returning it to the pan.

  5. Add Cream and Cashews

    6. Stir in the cashew paste. This acts as the natural thickener and gives the sauce its signature "silky" body. Follow this by adding the cream. Stir well to combine.

  6. Combine and Finish

    7. Add the cooked chicken (along with any juices on the plate) back into the sauce. Simmer for another 5-7 minutes so the chicken absorbs the flavors. Finally, sprinkle the crushed kasoori methi and add a teaspoon of sugar to balance the tanginess of the tomatoes.

  7. Serve

    8. Garnish with a final swirl of cream and serve hot with naan, roti, or steamed basmati rice.
