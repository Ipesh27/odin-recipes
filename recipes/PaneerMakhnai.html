Paneer Makhani Recipe

Ingredients

    For the Paneer Preparation

  • Paneer: 250g-300g, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • Warm Water: 1 bowl (to soak the paneer to keep it soft).
  • Optional Coating: A light dusting of turmeric and Kashmiri chili powder for the paneer cubes.

    For the Makhani Gravy

  • Butter: 3 tablespoons.
  • Oil: 1 tablespoon.
  • Whole Spices: 1 bay leaf, 1-inch cinnamon stick, 2 green cardamoms.
  • Onion: 1 medium, finely chopped (or made into a smooth paste for a silkier texture).
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste: 1 tablespoon.
  • Tomato Puree: 1.5 cups (freshly blended tomatoes yield the best flavor).
  • Cashew Paste: 1 tablespoon (soak 8-10 cashews in hot water and blend until smooth—this provides the signature restaurant-style richness).
  • Spices: 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp coriander powder.
  • Sugar/Honey: 1-2 teaspoons.
  • Heavy Cream: 1/4 to 1/2 cup.
  • Dried Fenugreek Leaves (Kasuri Methi): 1 tablespoon, crushed.
  • Salt: To taste.

Step-by-Step Recipe

  • Prepare the Paneer

    • Soak the paneer cubes in a bowl of warm water for 10–15 minutes. This ensures the paneer stays soft throughout the cooking process. Drain and pat dry just before adding to the gravy.

  • Sauté the Aromatics

    • Heat the butter and oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom) and let them sizzle for 30 seconds. Add the onions and sauté until golden brown. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.

  • Cook the Tomato Base

    • Add the tomato puree to the pan. Cook on medium heat until the mixture thickens and you see oil separating from the sides. Add the spice powders (chili, turmeric, coriander) and salt, mixing well for another minute.

  • Add the Richness

    • Stir in the cashew paste and cook for 2 minutes. If the gravy is too thick, add a small splash of water to reach your desired consistency.

  • Simmer

    • Add the paneer cubes to the gravy. Mix gently to ensure the paneer is coated without breaking it. Cover and simmer on low heat for 3–4 minutes, allowing the paneer to absorb the flavors.

  • The Finishing Touch

    • Stir in the heavy cream and sugar/honey to balance the acidity of the tomatoes. Sprinkle the crushed Kasuri Methi and a pinch of garam masala. Give it one final, gentle stir and turn off the heat immediately.

  • Serve

    • Garnish with a drizzle of fresh cream or a small cube of butter. Serve hot with garlic naan, laccha paratha, or jeera rice.
